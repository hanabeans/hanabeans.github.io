Hello! Welcome to the first blog post!

This one's gonna be something more... hobby-related? Personal, I guess?

And because I'll be mentioning their stuff a lot, no, I am (unfortunately) not sponsored by Aoshima...


I was at an auto show a couple of weeks ago. This was a much smaller one compared to the one I usually visit, though interestingly this one had a lot more hobby vendors around. And for once, they weren't just selling insanely overpriced 1:18 AutoArt scale models... in fact, a lot of them were actually reasonably priced. Caught me by complete surprise.


I then noticed one of the vendors was a model kit shop; not the mini4WD or even full-size R/C kind, but the static (usually 1:24) display models. Something like this, give or take. No motorization or other means of play here, they're just meant to be admired on a shelf, provided you can nail the details, paint, assembly, or even mods right. But when you do...




Yeah, so these static models are one of my severe weaknesses... I used to fawn over the whole building process for these things, and I even built some, although given I was like... 10? at the time, very poorly. The last one I built was a... Fujimi model Pajero Mini I think? I actually did an okay job setting the decals on that one, but I made the fatal mistake of only giving about 3 hours between coats of paint... the end result was orange peel galore. It was a pretty nice shade of red, but the car was basically completely ruined (orz). Never tried these proper model kits after that.


That was, until I came across these Aoshima snap-kit things about a year ago.

I've been aware of their existence since they came out back in what, 2018? or so? with the Suzuki Hustler and that W50 Prius model... the wheels were straight up stickers back then, and they've definitely come a long way since. I never really had the motivation nor desire to actually go get one for myself though, until I came across a shop that sold a bunch of these at an Aeon somewhere. Ended up getting an R33 GT-R in purple, feel free to call me boring.


The building process was... actually really refreshing (except for the damn window border stickers), the finished car looked pretty good, and the not-waterslide stickers not only looked surprisingly convincing, but they also actually stuck to the body... needless to say, I got hooked. Too bad the Aeon in question didn't really stock much of my interest at the time, other than a black GR86 which I was really tempted to get and slap one of my Million Live itashas on... somehow... I can only dream...


But back on topic, this particular vendor actually stocked the newer release snap-kits, like the Sambar, JA11 Jimny, the FD RX-7 model I was eyeing for quite a while...



I only make great financial investments.

It's even the Montego Blue colour that I really like...


I haven't actually built the car yet, since I've still got finals in a couple of weeks, so we'll see how that goes. Just figured I wanted to share one of the last things that keeps me happy for once (damn that's insanely pathetic). That said, I couldn't resist taking the body out, though, and getting a rough idea of what the finished car would look like.



It kinda looks like a C5 Corvette like this, doesn't it...?


Given it's a pre-painted model, the body colour isn't as accurate as my painted Tomica Limited Vintage model of the same car (which is incredibly hard to get the right shade of on camera), but it looks close enough. I do find it rather interesting how the ɛ̃fini and RX-7 (rear) emblems are raised off the body itself, so you could ditch the stickers it came with and paint them yourself instead, if you wanted to. The ability to pick between open or closed retractable lights is also a very nice feature, although unfortunately you can't actually flip the lights up and down, as it instead just has separate pieces for an opened headlight and a closed headlight.




I'm really looking forward to building this one. The FD is just drop dead gorgeous, even if it might be a bit overrated. It's also surprisingly hard to find scale models of these stock, pre-facelift FDs (which I do prefer over the facelift series 8s, call me weird), so having one in this scale is hella nice. I do hope they'll make an RX-8 one next though, especially if it's a facelift one, because genuinely no one except Tomica makes models of those... and to further fuel people's thoughts that I might be clinically insane, the RX-8 is also my actual dream car as opposed to the 7...


Or maybe they should also make some kei cars... I'd kill for a model of a 2nd gen Honda N-One or Suzuki Spacia Gear.


On a complete side note, I've been using Imgur for the images in this blog due to their filesizes. I should consider self-hosting one day though (which is partially why I started this Neocities page in the first place, to see what I would do with a domain), since there's no guarantee these images will stay up for... God knows how long, really.

We'll see if I can make the most out of that domain if I ever decide to buy one, because I don't want to just restrict is as an image host/file dump/etc...


Sorry if the writing for this one was especially bad... it's 0:30 and I've been running on 3 hours of sleep since... 5 am yesterday...
